## Ask the health question directly

The letter carries the standard adtech asks — hashed match keys with the
suppression-versus-inventory distinction, device and CTV identifiers, **the
edges** rather than just the rows, IP-derived household association, segment
memberships as inferences rather than facts.

The addition worth reusing is a **single direct question about health-related
segments**: any audience or inference relating to a condition, treatment,
medication, diagnosis, or a condition inferred from browsing or app behaviour,
including audiences built for pharmaceutical, provider or health-service
advertisers.

Asked explicitly for two reasons, both stated in the letter: a general assurance
is least informative in exactly this category, and health-adjacent audiences are
routinely described as "de-identified" while remaining targetable at an
individual device. The framing offers the easy answer — *"if you hold nothing of
the sort, that is a one-line answer and I will accept it"* — which is what makes
a plain reply cheap to give.

The browser-opt-out deflection is pre-empted rather than waited for:
device-scoped, dies when storage is cleared or the device is replaced, does
nothing about server-side records keyed to a hashed email.
