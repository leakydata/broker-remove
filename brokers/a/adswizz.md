## Audio has two properties a generic deletion request misses

**Listening history is sensitive by inference.** What someone listens to can
indicate health conditions, religion, politics, recovery or support-group
participation, and sexuality — none of it declared. The letter names this as the
part that matters most and asks whether episode- or station-level history is
associated with the subject.

**Audio targeting is frequently cookieless.** Server-side ad insertion keys on an
IP address, a device-graph entry, or a publisher-supplied identifier. Two
consequences: a cookie opt-out does not touch it, and a name-keyed search returns
nothing while a full profile exists.

The letter pre-empts the browser-opt-out deflection rather than waiting for it:
device-scoped, dies when storage is cleared, does nothing about server-side
records keyed to a hashed email or an IP. See `_DEFLECTIONS.md`.
