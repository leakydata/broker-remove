## Gotchas

**The auto-reply is also a retention pitch.** After naming the removal form and
the privacy policy, the same message adds: "proceeding with this request might
mean your business or organization will risk losing future relevant business
opportunities... If you would prefer not to miss out... please update your
information [instead]." Read it as a soft upsell/retention nudge attached to an
otherwise standard auto-ack, not as a condition on the request — it does not
require a reply and does not change what was asked for.
