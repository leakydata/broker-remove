## The copies already sold are the ones that ring the phone

Same shape as `all_web_leads.md`. A lead is sold, often repeatedly, sometimes to
buyers who resell it — so deleting the source record removes the least
consequential copy. The asks are built around that:

- **Sale count and buyers**, or the count and buyer categories if a full list is
  impossible.
- **Direct the buyers to delete.** Without this the deletion is nominal.
- **Where the lead originated** — which site, which date, or which affiliate.
  Lead gen runs through affiliate networks where the collection point is several
  hops upstream and unreachable without a name.
- **Aged-lead resale**, where a record circulates years after any interest
  expired.
- **Do-not-call held independently of the lead record**, so deleting the record
  does not delete the protection. Prior numbers matter as much as current ones: a
  reassigned number that still resolves to the subject attaches their name to a
  stranger's line, which is worse than an accurate listing.
- **Category attributes** — insurance status, vehicles, property, health, age
  band, household finances. Lead forms collect well beyond contact details and
  those fields travel with the lead.
