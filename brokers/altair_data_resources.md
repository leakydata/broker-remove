## Direct mail: the copies already deployed are the ones that matter

Where the model is list *rental* rather than transfer, deleting the master leaves
every deployed copy intact — and those generate the mail. So the letter asks for
a count of rentals and, as far as possible, the recipients.

**`do-not-mail` is requested as a standing entry alongside do-not-sell.** Postal
mail is the output here and the one that keeps arriving after everything else is
settled.

**The appended and modelled attributes are the real payload**: estimated income
or net worth, home ownership, presence of children, age band, purchase and
donation history, lifestyle codes, geodemographic cluster. Largely inferences,
invisible to the subject, and the part with commercial value. A deletion that
clears the name row while modelled attributes persist against the *household* has
kept the product.

Sent to a named individual because that is the contact nominated in the state
filing, with a request to forward if privacy is handled elsewhere — a registered
contact is a legal designation, not necessarily the right desk.
