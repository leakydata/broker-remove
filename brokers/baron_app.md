## Gotchas

- **The Candl/Cameo registration confusion is real and worth knowing before
  writing.** Cameo's own reply: the CA broker registration under "Baron
  App, Inc." was triggered by **Candl**, a discontinued mobile app — not by
  Cameo. Cameo says Candl's data has been deleted. States that require
  broker registration typically don't allow deregistering mid-year even
  after the triggering business is shut down, so the stale-looking
  registration is a registry-timing artifact, not evidence Cameo itself is
  still doing whatever Candl did. **Don't assume the registration describes
  Cameo's current data practices — ask, as this letter did, rather than
  asserting it.**
- **"We only process the sending address" reads like an anti-fraud policy,
  not a hostile refusal** — but applied to someone who lists several of
  their own addresses, it forces either N separate emails (impractical when
  you don't control mailboxes for all N) or routes the rest through an
  "authorized agent" process that doesn't fit a first-party multi-address
  request either. Push back once, plainly: *I am the consumer, these are
  all my addresses, and I'm confirming ownership from the address that sent
  the original request* — see reply text in the thread.
- **The "My Privacy Choices" cookie-based opt-out is a different scope**
  from an identifier-based deletion request. It stops advertising use tied
  to a browser/cookie; it does not search or delete a record keyed to an
  email address or name. Don't let a company substitute one for the other
  without saying so.
- Safety-mailbox routing: if your request lands at a `safety@` address
  because that's what a registry filing lists, say so in the first line —
  it's likely triaged against genuinely urgent reports and privacy requests
  arriving there may be lower priority than they'd be at a dedicated
  privacy inbox.
