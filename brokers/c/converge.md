- Note: FOLLOW-UP BUILT ENTIRELY ON THEIR OWN REGISTER FILING, and the most serious single disclosure found so far. Converge Direct's 2025 filing says: 'Converge may collect consumer health data in connection with its provision of LEAD GENERATION SERVICES TO ITS LAW FIRM CLIENTS, INCLUDING REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH DATA. The nature of any consumer health data collection varies based on the objectives of the relevant law firms.' That is mass-tort lead generation keyed to health conditions, self-disclosed on a state filing. OPENED BY GIVING THEM CREDIT, and meant it: most registrants leave that box empty and several who plainly do collect health data answered no. They did not. Said explicitly I was not writing to punish a company for being candid on a form. ADDED 1798.121 -- limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information -- which my original request had not asked for because I did not know it applied. Health and reproductive health data are sensitive PI under 1798.140(ae); the limit right is distinct from deletion and opt-out and is not satisfied by either. THE QUESTION THAT MATTERS IS PROVENANCE, AND IT IS A MORAL DISTINCTION NOT JUST A LEGAL ONE: did the health attribute come from (a) a person filling in a form about their own condition, knowing they were answering a law firm advertisement, or (b) an attribute APPENDED OR INFERRED from purchased data, web behaviour, modelled segments or location signals? In (a) the person knows what they disclosed. In (b) THEY DO NOT KNOW THE RECORD EXISTS, HAVE NEVER SEEN IT, AND CANNOT CORRECT IT -- and an inference about reproductive health can be wrong about a person in ways that are not recoverable. Both are personal information (1798.140(v)(1)(K) covers inferences explicitly) but they are not the same thing. Also asked which CATEGORIES OF CONDITION are targeted -- not clients, categories, which 1798.110 entitles me to anyway -- and whether health data already disclosed to a law firm client was deleted there too under 1798.105(c), since a lead that has been sold does not come back, and this is the category where a stray copy does the most harm. Re-stated the identifiers because their auto-reply had offered direct assistance if the form could not be used, and I took that offer on 25 Aug with no answer in a week.
