## Gotchas

They answered, and the answer was unusually clear:

> *"does not own, store, or publish any personal records, background reports, or
> criminal-history information. We are an informational website and keep no
> database of individuals, so there is nothing on our end to remove or delete."*

Take that at face value, because it is checkable and because they volunteered the
next step: they named TruthFinder as the provider their searches reach.

**This is the most useful thing a front site can do.** A referral shopfront holds
nothing, so a request filed against it removes nothing — but the same request
makes the operator tell you where the data actually lives. That converts a dead
end into a lead. TruthFinder is part of PeopleConnect and is submitted separately;
see `peopleconnect.md`.

Two things worth keeping in mind before recording this as settled:

- **"We hold no database" is a claim about now.** An affiliate that later starts
  caching search results is a different company from the one that wrote this.
  The statement is evidence, dated, not a permanent exemption.
- **They offered to review a specific page.** If a search result naming the
  subject ever surfaces on this domain, that offer is the route to use, and their
  own statement is the leverage — a page that names someone contradicts "we keep
  no database of individuals".

`not_found` here means *confirmed in writing that they hold nothing*. Do not
reuse that status for a broker that merely failed to find a record; those are
different findings and conflating them costs a re-check later.
