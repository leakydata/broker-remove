## Steps

1. Email `support@callapp.com`. Lead with "I am not a user of this app" —
   a caller-ID service's default support flow assumes an account holder,
   and a non-user request needs to be flagged immediately or it risks
   being triaged as an account-recovery question.
2. Ask them to search and remove any entry keyed to the phone number(s)
   listed, regardless of which installer's upload originally contributed
   it.
