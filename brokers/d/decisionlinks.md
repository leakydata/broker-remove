  surface it is worth asking which of them were run.

## Two sentences, and the second one is the durable half (updated 2026-08-19)

> "To whom it may concern,
>
> We have no data on this individual and we have added them to our suppression
> list."

Recorded `not_found`. No account, no form, no ID document, no profile URL, and no
argument about jurisdiction — two days from letter to answer.

> **"We hold nothing" and "we hold nothing *and will not accept you in future*"
> are different answers.** The first expires the moment a new feed arrives. The
> second is a standing rule. Both are one sentence to write, and the operators who
> volunteer the second are describing a suppression list they already run.

The same shape appeared this week from an unrelated group — *"Your information was
not found in our system, and it has been added to our CCPA Opt-outs to keep your
information out of our system in the future"* — which suggests this is simply what
a competently-run B2B data business does when asked, rather than anything special
about either company. It is worth asking for explicitly at the ones that only give
you the first half.
