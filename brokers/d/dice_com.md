- Note: 2026-08-25: emailed support@dice.com. NEW CATEGORY - job board / resume database, and the framing that matters is that Dice sells RECRUITER ACCESS to a searchable candidate database, so a profile is a product rather than a service. Four asks: (1) the resume itself, which is one of the densest personal records a person ever creates - employment, education, dates, skills, sometimes address and phone; (2) records created WITHOUT an application - sourced, enriched or inferred profiles, plus derived first.last@employer addresses using the Kaspr admission; (3) RECRUITER SEARCH AND VIEW HISTORY - has my profile been returned in searches or viewed, how often, over what period, explicitly NOT asking which recruiter or employer, on the same ask-whether-not-who principle that works for skip tracing; (4) inferred attributes - skill inference, seniority, salary estimate, and job-change-likelihood or 'open to opportunities' scores, noting that a prediction someone is likely to leave their job is a record they never agreed to, cannot see, and that could cause real harm if it reached the wrong reader. Leaned on the closed .edu address as a search key since an old job-board account is keyed to whatever email was current when created.
