## Gotchas

Writing to a customer-service mailbox is worth doing, but say in the letter that
you know that is what it is. Two reasons: it makes it easy for the recipient to
route rather than to close the ticket as misdirected, and asking them to name the
right contact turns a low-quality route into a good one for next time.

Otherwise this is the standard direct-mail and fulfilment case: files indexed on
address history, rebuilt on a cycle. **Suppression, not deletion** — see
`dmdatabases_com.md`, where the same point is worked through — and ask which
mailers or clients received the data, since a list already out of the door is not
recalled by deleting the master copy.
