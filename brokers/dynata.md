## Gotchas

Two things make market research different from a people-search site.

**The record may not be keyed to your name.** Panel data is commonly held against
a panellist ID, with the identifying details in a separate table. A name search
can therefore come back empty while the response data — which is the sensitive
part, since it is what you told them about yourself — remains. Ask for a search by
identifier, and ask what identifier they hold you under.

**The profile is the product.** Demographic, attitudinal and behavioural profiles
are personal information about you even where they were inferred rather than
collected. A deletion that removes the identifying fields and keeps the profile
has removed the cheap half.

Also worth asking: research panels frequently operate on behalf of clients, so the
same data may sit in a client's system under a licence. Ask who, and ask them to
direct those recipients to delete it.

## Verification

No public listing. Ask for the categories held at deletion, the panels involved,
and the client recipients notified — that disclosure is the only visibility
available from outside.
