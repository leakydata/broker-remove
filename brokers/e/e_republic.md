## Gotchas

Trade publishing in a government market is a data business wearing a media
business's clothes. The subscriber file *is* the product sold to sponsors, and the
records arrive from three different directions, only one of which the person
remembers:

- **Things you signed up for** — a subscription, a webinar, a whitepaper download.
  These are findable under your personal email, and a name search will surface
  them.
- **Things compiled from public rosters.** Public-sector audience data is
  frequently built from government directories and official listings rather than
  from anything submitted. Such a record may be held under a **job title and an
  official address**, with no personal identifier attached — so a search of your
  home address and personal email returns nothing while the record sits there.
  Say the role in the letter and ask them to search on that basis.
- **Things a sponsor gave them**, or that they gave a sponsor.

**The sponsor question is the one that outlives the deletion.** Registration data
shared with an event sponsor is a copy in somebody else's CRM, and deleting the
publisher's record does not touch it. Ask which sponsors received it.
