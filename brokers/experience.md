## Gotchas

A review platform holds three quite different things, and a single deletion
request usually reaches only the first:

- **Content the person submitted** — ratings, reviews, survey responses.
- **A profile the platform published about them**, which they may never have
  created.
- **A contact record a business customer uploaded** so that a feedback request
  could be sent. This is the one the person has no way of knowing exists.

**"Published anonymously" does not mean not personal.** A review displayed under
a first name and a last initial is personal information if the platform's own
systems can join it back to an identity — and they can, because that is how the review was collected. Say
so, so that anonymised display is not offered as a reason the request does not
apply.

Expect the **processor deflection** (`_DEFLECTIONS.md` §21): we hold this for our
business customers. Ask for the customer name in the same breath, so a refusal at
least yields the next address to write to.

Ask whether the deletion is a suppression against re-addition. Clients re-upload
contact lists on a cycle; a one-time deletion means the record returns at the next
upload, having been deleted exactly as asked.
