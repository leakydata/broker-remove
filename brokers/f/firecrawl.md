## Gotchas

- **The published `privacy@` address doesn't exist; the support inbox does.** A bounce is invisible to the recipient — from inside, `privacy@firecrawl.dev` "looks like one nobody uses" because no failure ever reaches them. Anyone who wrote there without also copying `support@firecrawl.dev`/`help@firecrawl.com` gets silently dropped. Always CC the general support address when the dedicated privacy address is unverified.
- **Not a data broker — a downstream customer.** Firecrawl showed up here only because a supplier (FullEnrich) named them, in writing, as the client who ran a reverse-lookup. Worth the reminder that a compiled-data trail can lead to ordinary SaaS companies, not just brokers, and that those companies are often more responsive than the brokers because they have nothing to hide.
- **"We never enrich any address" is a plausible, checkable claim, not a dodge.** Firecrawl's account-deletion flow and its enrichment-supplier relationship are different systems; a rep who can speak confidently to "we never enrich" while still deleting an unrelated stray account is a coherent story, not a contradiction. Don't manufacture a gotcha out of two true statements that describe different parts of the business.
