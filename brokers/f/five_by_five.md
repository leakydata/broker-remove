## Gotchas

The name and structure suggest a **co-operative or consortium**: members
contribute data into a shared pool and draw from it. If so, that is the single
most important thing to establish, because it determines what a deletion can even
mean.

In a pooled arrangement there are at least three distinct copies — the pool, each
contributing member's own file, and each drawing member's export — and a deletion
at the co-operative touches only the first. A confirmation reading "your data has
been deleted" is then entirely true and leaves the record in place at every member
who ever received it.

So ask which one you are getting, in those terms: *does this apply to the pooled
copy, to the member contributions, or only to your own records?* And ask for the
member list, since without it there is no way to pursue the rest.

This is the co-operative form of the processor deflection (`_DEFLECTIONS.md` §21)
and the same reply applies: if you cannot delete it, name who can.
