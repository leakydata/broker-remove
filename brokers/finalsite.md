- Note: Directory-sourced, and the first letter where I told a company outright that I think the listing is PROBABLY WRONG. Finalsite provides websites, communications and enrolment software to schools, so on the ordinary description it holds student, parent and staff data AS A PROCESSOR ON A SCHOOL'S INSTRUCTIONS -- close to the opposite of a broker, whose defining act is selling information about people it has no relationship with. Said 'that listing is not accurate, we are a school software vendor' is a complete answer. THE SUBSTANTIVE QUESTION, asked as a question and not an allegation: IS SCHOOL DATA EVER USED BEYOND THE SCHOOL'S OWN INSTRUCTIONS -- product analytics, cross-district benchmarking, aggregate research, model training, or an enrolment/marketing product built on the aggregate. The processor boundary is where school platforms actually get into difficulty and it is invisible from outside: a school signs a contract, a parent never sees one, and the people whose data it is have no relationship with the vendor, cannot know what the contract says, and could not exercise a right without going through the school. And the population is CHILDREN -- said plainly that I am not alleging anything improper, only that it is the category where a quiet secondary use would matter most and be noticed least. THREE LIMITS: asking about ME only; NOT asking them to touch a school's records or act outside a processor mandate (name the school instead and I will approach them); and NOT asking about anybody else's record -- if a search on my identifiers surfaces someone else's data, tell me rather than act, least of all a minor's. Identifier set deliberately trimmed -- no postal history, since an education platform is unlikely to be address-keyed and I would rather not add data they do not need, with the 222 escape hatch attached. psu.edu flagged as the one most likely to appear in an education-sector system.
