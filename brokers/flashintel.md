## Gotchas

A B2B contact database will not find you from a home address, so a consumer-shaped
letter gets a truthful "no record" while a full contact record sits under a work
email and a job title. Give them the professional keys.

**The export question is the one that matters most here.** These services exist to
be exported: a customer runs a search, downloads a list, and the contact lands in
their CRM. That copy is not reached by any deletion at the source, and it is the
copy that will call you. Ask who has it — and note that a deletion which does not
address exports is, in practice, a deletion of the least consequential copy.

Also worth noting for the registry: `myflashcloud.com` is the published privacy
domain for the FlashIntel brand. Cross-domain privacy addresses are a family
signal (`_BROKER_FAMILIES.md`) — here it is the same operator under two names
rather than two companies.
