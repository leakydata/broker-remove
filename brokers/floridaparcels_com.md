## Gotchas

**Search by address.** A parcel site is address-keyed: a name search can
truthfully return nothing while a parcel page still names the subject as an owner,
former owner or resident. Give them every address and ask which returned a record.

**The association is the request, not the record.** The underlying parcel data is
public and is not going anywhere — asking a private site to delete a county
assessor's record is asking for something they cannot do, and invites a refusal
that sounds reasonable. What they *can* do, and what actually matters, is stop
republishing the join between a name and a parcel in a form that is searchable by
name. Say that is what you are asking for; it is both more likely to be granted
and more useful when it is.

**Assessor data is re-ingested on a cycle**, so a one-time removal is temporary by
construction and the confirmation looks identical either way. Ask which you got.

The contact address sits on a different domain, which is the usual sign of a
shared operator running several property-records sites (`_BROKER_FAMILIES.md`).
Ask them to name the siblings — one letter can cover all of them.
