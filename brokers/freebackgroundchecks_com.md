## Gotchas

Standard background-check shape — see `fastbackgroundcheck.md` for the disclosure
and FCRA arguments worked through at length.

**The name collision is worth asking about.** `freebackgroundcheck.org` (singular,
`.org`) is a different domain with two hard-bouncing addresses and a privacy policy
whose every link points at archive.org snapshots — it has no working removal route
at all (`freebackgroundcheck_org.md`).

Whether the two are related is unknown, and that is exactly why to ask. If they
share an index, one letter should cover both and the working contact becomes the
route for the broken one. If they are unrelated, the answer costs nothing and
removes a false lead.

**Near-identical names are a weak family signal but a cheap question.** Unlike a
shared privacy address, a similar domain proves nothing — but asking the operator
directly is one sentence, and the reply is authoritative in a way no amount of
WHOIS inspection would be.

## Verification

Re-run the public search on each former address and each old number. Check a
relative's listing for the subject's name in the related-persons block. If they
confirm a relationship to the `.org` site, verify there too — a shared index means
a removal at one should be visible at the other, and if it is not, the removal was
narrower than claimed.
