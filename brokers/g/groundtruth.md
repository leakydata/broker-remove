## Gotchas

The standard location-broker shape is in `factori.md` and `foursquare.md`:
device-keyed index, observations versus mappings, do not hand over a MAID before
asking what they can search on.

**The addition that belongs on every visitation-data letter is sensitive
locations.** A store-visit dataset is not only a record of shopping. The same
observations reveal attendance at medical facilities, places of worship, legal and
financial offices, shelters, treatment centres and political events — and firms in
this business have historically packaged exactly those as audience segments.

A request framed as "delete my personal information" will not reach a segment
called something like *recent urgent-care visitor*. Nobody is hiding it; it simply
is not what the phrase brings to mind, for the requester or the responder. So name
the category, and ask for deletion of any such segment specifically.

Two reasons this is worth its own paragraph rather than a clause:

- It tells the responder to look in the segment tables, not just the identity
  tables.
- If they hold nothing of the kind, saying so is easy and the answer is worth
  having in writing.
