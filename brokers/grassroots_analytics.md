## Gotchas

**Political affiliation is a sensitive attribute, and suppression does not remove
it.** Giving history, partisanship, ideology, issue interests, wealth estimates and
propensity-to-give scores are the product here. A do-not-contact flag stops the
calls and leaves every one of those attributes sitting on a record that remains
licensable. Ask for the attributes to be deleted, in those words, and say why the
distinction matters.

**Pre-empt the public-source argument.** Some contribution records are public
filings, and "it's public information" is the natural reply. Concede the part that
is true and restate the part that is not:

> *"I am not asking you to alter a public filing, which you could not do. I am
> asking you to delete your compiled record, your derived scores, and your
> association of those filings with the rest of my identifying information."*

That distinction is the whole substance of the request. A public filing sitting in
a government database and a commercial profile that joins it to eight email
addresses, twelve phone numbers and a wealth score are different objects, and only
the second is theirs to delete.

Everything else is standard: permanent suppression rather than deletion (these
files rebuild from voter files and public filings on a cycle), client
distribution, and — per `_SILENT_FAILURES.md` §16 — suppression entries that
survive the deletion rather than being removed with the record.
