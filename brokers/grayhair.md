## Gotchas

Mail-tracking and delivery analytics is a delivery-point-keyed business, so the
address history is the index that matters and a name search may find nothing at
all. Lead with the addresses.

**Two categories worth naming, because neither is what "my personal information"
brings to mind:**

- **Mail-piece and scan records.** Delivery confirmations, induction records, scan
  events and mail-piece histories addressed to a delivery point. Reasonably
  linkable to the person who lives there, whatever field the name sits in.
- **Move, vacancy and occupancy data.** Change-of-address and address-validation
  records. **A move history is one of the most complete pictures of a life a
  commercial dataset can hold** — dates, sequence, duration at each address — and
  it is the last thing anyone pictures when they think about mail software. Ask
  about it explicitly.

Expect the processor position: the data belongs to mailer clients. Ask for the
client list in the same breath, so a refusal at least names who to write to next.
