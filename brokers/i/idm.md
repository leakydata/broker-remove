## Gotchas

**A `ccpa@` mailbox is a scope claim.** It tells a consumer outside California,
without anyone having to say it or defend it, that the channel is not for them —
and most will not write. See `_DEFLECTIONS.md` §23 and `freewheel.md`, where the
same arrangement produced an accepted request from a Pennsylvania resident once it
was simply challenged in the opening paragraph.

The move costs a paragraph: write anyway, say you are aware of the name, ask them
not to treat it as the scope of their obligations, and ask for any residency-based
refusal in writing. That converts a silent deterrent into either an actioned
request or a documented position.

Everything else is the standard data-provider shape — hashed identifiers
(`email_industries.md`), suppression rather than deletion (`dmdatabases_com.md`),
and derived attributes as distinct from source fields (`fourleaf.md`).
