## Gotchas

The standard adtech asks apply (`disco_network.md`, `evorra.md`): hashed
identifiers, the linkages rather than the node, downstream activation, and the
opt-out-versus-deletion distinction stated up front rather than waiting to be given
the wrong one.

**The addition specific to a journey-based platform is sequence state.** Their
product advertises to a person across a designed sequence — which means they hold
not only *who you are* but *where in a journey you are*: which advertisements you
have already been shown, what you did next, which step comes now.

That state is personal information about the person, and it is the thing most
likely to survive a deletion, because a request framed around *identifiers* reads
as being about identifiers. Ask for it by name.
