## Gotchas

Standard people-search shape — `fastpeoplesearch_io.md` covers the two recurring
gaps at length.

**The addition here is refusing the paywall in advance.** Some sites in this
category route removals through a flow that requires an account, a purchase or a
subscription — which turns exercising a deletion right into becoming a customer of
the company you are asking to forget you.

State up front that you will not do that. It costs nothing, it is usually
unnecessary, and where it is necessary the refusal is worth having in writing: a
company that will only delete your data if you buy something has said something
useful about itself.
