PUT THAT AGAINST §333: their form, with Pennsylvania selected, offered EXACTLY ONE request type -- Do not Sell or Share. That is a designated-methods problem rather than a refusal: nothing here says no, and yet a consumer who does not telephone cannot obtain a deletion.
