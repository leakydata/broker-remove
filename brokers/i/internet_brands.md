## Status

- Current: `submitted` (updated 2026-08-28)
- Note: Sent a GROUP-SCOPE variant, same shape as the Informa letter. Internet Brands runs many consumer-facing properties under their own names and privacy notices, several collecting personal data directly, so the letter opens on scope: cover every property, name which ones were searched, or say if per-brand requests are required and name the brands. The line put to them plainly -- from outside, a partial answer and a complete one look identical, and on a group this size that is the difference between a resolved request and an unresolved one the subject cannot detect.

Two categories flagged as the ones a group-level search tends to miss: (1) LEAD-GENERATION RECORDS -- a quote, consultation, valuation or callback request supplied onward to a professional or vendor, with the ask being WHICH PROPERTY it came from and WHO it went to, since a lead is frequently resold and the origin is the only way to stop it re-entering; (2) HEALTH-ADJACENT PROPERTIES, where the sensitive-data limit-use right is asked for alongside deletion, on the ground that an inferred health attribute is neither visible nor correctable by the person it describes and can simply be wrong. Carries the new 144 retention clause.
