## Gotchas

- **This is a suppression, not a deletion, and they say so.** Their own reply:
  *"This suppression request prevents those records from being displayed as
  required by applicable law, but does not delete or alter the underlying public
  record from its original source."* For bulk public-record-fed listings this is
  actually the right mechanism — a one-time deletion would reappear at the next
  import — but don't describe it as full deletion in any downstream report.
- They explicitly invite naming **additional** identifying info if something is
  still visible after suppression — worth following up rather than assuming one
  round is final.
- FCRA disclaimer boilerplate on every reply (not a consumer reporting agency) —
  expected, not a real deflection.
