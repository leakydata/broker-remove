## The address in their privacy policy does not exist

`ccpa@leadmemedia.com` -- the address Lead Me Media designates for consumer rights
requests -- rejects mail with *"Address not found"*.

That is worth more than a routing correction, and the resent letter says so
directly rather than quietly using the fallback:

> *"an address published for consumer rights requests, which silently rejects them,
> means every request anyone has sent there has been lost, and each sender has no
> way to tell that apart from being ignored."*

The only working address published anywhere on the site is `info@leadmemedia.com`,
on the homepage. The privacy policy itself lives on a separate host
(`policy.leadmemedia.com`) and renders no contact address that a fetch can see.

**Report the dead mailbox as a fault, in the same message.** It costs one paragraph.
A company that has not noticed is more likely to fix it than to argue, and if they
do fix it, everyone who writes to them afterwards benefits -- which is the point of
doing this in the open.
