## Gotchas

- **Itemised per-identifier references are the tell of a real search.** "No records found" alone reads identically whether a company searched everything or one field; a reference number per identifier searched (even with one duplicate reference in the batch, worth a follow-up question but not a red flag on its own) lets you see the coverage rather than infer it. One of the more genuinely useful reply formats seen in this project.
- **A "current identifiers only" nil is not the same result as a "full identifier history" nil.** The first letter here only listed current emails/address; the meaningful negative only arrived after prior/dead addresses and phone numbers were supplied on request.
