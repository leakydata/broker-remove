## Closed as not_found, and why that was the right call

Their first reply was a canned negative — *"we did not identify any information
in our databases associated with your name and/or address"* — and the narrow
wording invited a reading that only name and address had been searched. That
reading was wrong. Asked about it, they answered in six minutes:

> *"the response is a standard email reply, so I can understand the confusion. To
> clarify, we did check our records and client records we hold for all of those
> email addresses and..."*

**A canned negative under-describes the search that was actually run.** It is
evidence about the template, not about what the analyst did. See
`_SILENT_FAILURES.md` §52 and its correction — this is the case that produced it.

Register was what made the difference. *"Did you also search email and phone?"*
is a fair question. *"You only searched name and address"* would have been an
accusation, and it would have been false.

**The agency question is still the right one to ask** of any firm that holds data
on behalf of clients: an agency can truthfully hold nothing in its own systems
while a client file contains you. Here the answer was that both were searched.
