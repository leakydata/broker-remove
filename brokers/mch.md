## An institutional file is keyed to your work life, not your home

MCH compiles contact data on people in institutional settings — education, healthcare
and similar. A record about a person there is likely filed under a **former employer or
institution**, and keyed to a **work address, a job title, or an institutional email
address** rather than a residential one.

That has a direct consequence for the identifier list: a `.edu` address is exactly the
sort of key such a file retains, and a search limited to home address and personal
webmail would miss it entirely while returning a perfectly honest "no record found".

The letter therefore asks two things a generic one would not: search on the
institutional identifiers as well, and — if they treat a professional record as
*business* rather than *consumer* data — say so explicitly and name the basis, rather
than declining without explanation.
