# Melissa (Melissa Data)

- **Privacy:** https://www.melissa.com/company/data-privacy
- **Email:** consumerrequest@melissa.com (verified — a real request address, not
  a guessed `privacy@`)
- **Method:** email
- **Priority: 4.**

## Not a people-search site

Melissa sells **data quality and address verification** services. Your details are
likely to sit in *reference datasets licensed to their customers* rather than on a
public profile page. That changes what to ask for:

- Confirm whether your information appears in any licensed reference dataset
- Apply deletion and suppression **there**, not only in any consumer-facing lookup
- Ask what happens to copies already delivered to licensees

A removal that only clears a public lookup tool while leaving you in the licensed
data product is not a removal.

## Gotchas
- There is no public listing to re-check, so their written confirmation is the
  only verification available. Insist on it.
- See `_CATEGORY_VARIANTS.md` for the general shape of requests to B2B data
  vendors.
