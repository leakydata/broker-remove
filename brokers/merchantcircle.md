- Note: Directory-sourced. MerchantCircle is a local BUSINESS directory, not a people-search, so the letter leads with why a business directory is being asked at all: small-business listings are very often a person wearing a company's hat -- the 'business phone' is a mobile, the 'business address' is a home, the owner name is the person's name. What gets compiled, enriched and licensed onward is a named individual's HOME ADDRESS AND PERSONAL PHONE, filed as business data. LOAD-BEARING LEGAL POINT: California's B2B exemption EXPIRED 1 JANUARY 2023. Personal info collected in a commercial or employment context has since been ordinary personal information with the same rights attached -- a listing does not stop being about a person because it is filed under a trade name. This is the one argument that unlocks directory rows and the old 'that's B2B data' answer no longer holds. Asked deletion incl. anything held inside a MERCHANT record rather than a consumer record; opt-out incl. licensing to list brokers and lead-gen buyers; categories disclosure. Two decisive questions: (1) listing URL for any page naming me as owner, contact, reviewer or claimed-business account (SF 185), with 'no such page' accepted as a good answer; (2) PROVENANCE BOTH WAYS -- directory listings are ingested from print yellow-pages files, licensed compilations and crawls, then syndicated outward, so deleting their copy while the upstream feed still carries it just means it returns. Suppress-not-delete + survival-of-migration ask, SF 193 address limit, common-name discriminator, SF 235 entity-name question.
