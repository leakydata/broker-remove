(1) THE FCRA FORK, which bites harder here than at an ordinary ad company because their product line includes a lending-directed offering: is any output, score, segment, feature or attribute used or expected to be used as a factor in eligibility for CREDIT, INSURANCE, EMPLOYMENT or HOUSING?
