## Gotchas

**Concede what cannot be won, immediately.** Deeds and assessment records are
public and not the republisher's to alter. Arguing otherwise wastes the letter and
invites a refusal that is correct.

**Then split hosting from findability**, which is the ask that can be won:
- hosting a public record is what the county already does;
- indexing it against a person's *name*, enriching it with telephone numbers and
  email addresses, ranking it into search results and offering it as a lead is a
  product the republisher built.

The second is theirs, and it is the part that makes a person findable.

**Ask for de-indexing as a separate item from deletion**, and ask for a `noindex`
directive specifically. A page whose content is removed but which remains indexed
stays findable for a long time, and "we removed it" will be said truthfully about
exactly that state.

**Say so when there is probably no match.** There is no New Jersey address
anywhere in this subject's history. Saying that plainly, and inviting an
unqualified "we hold nothing" as a complete answer, is faster than a removal
request that has nothing to remove — and it produces a cleaner record than
silence would.
