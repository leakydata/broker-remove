## One of the easiest brokers to action

Their support offers **three** ways to identify a record, and the third is unusual:

1. The profile URL (`nuwber.com/person/<id>`)
2. A **screenshot** of the page
3. **The phone number listed on the page**

Option 3 matters. Every other people-search site in this project required finding
a profile URL first — a search, a match against a common name, then extraction.
Nuwber will locate the record from a phone number alone, so supply every number
you have ever used and skip the lookup entirely. That is what worked here.

## What they will not tell you

They confirmed deletion but did **not** answer either follow-up question: how many
records matched, and whether the details are suppressed against future re-ingest.
Ask, but do not expect an answer — and re-check later rather than assuming a single
deletion covered every record.

## Search-engine caches outlive the removal

> *"The link to your deleted profile can still be visible in Google and other search
> engines. It may take them from several days to sometimes 8 weeks to remove this
> broken link. Please note that we have no control or influence over this process."*

This is honest and generalises: **a removal is not immediately invisible.** When
verifying, check the broker's own site, not a search engine. A cached result weeks
later is not evidence the removal failed.

To clear caches faster, use the search engines' own tools:
- Google: <https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/6349986>
- Bing: <https://www.bing.com/webmasters/tools/contentremoval>
