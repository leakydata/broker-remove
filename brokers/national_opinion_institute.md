A SURVEY RESEARCH AGENCY ON THE DATA BROKER REGISTER, and the letter is built on that tension: genuine opinion research produces aggregate insight and treats individual responses as confidential, while a broker registration means individual-level data leaving the building. Both can be true of different activities -- so ask which is the registered one. FOUR ASKS. (1) What is sold and about whom: survey PARTICIPANTS recruited through their panel, or people who never took part and were compiled from elsewhere? The subject is in the second category if anywhere. (2) THE SENSITIVE-DATA POINT, TAKEN FROM THEIR OWN SITE: their footer link reads 'Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information AND LIMIT THE USE OF MY SENSITIVE PERSONAL INFORMATION' -- and the limit-use right under 1798.121 is only offered by businesses that HOLD sensitive personal information. So which categories do they hold, and any about me? Opinion research reaches health, religion, sexual orientation, political belief and union membership routinely. Exercised the limit-use right AS A SEPARATE ITEM (numbered 3 in the request list) and asked for it answered separately from deletion. (3) SURVEY RESPONSES ARE ANSWERS, NOT ATTRIBUTES: asked whether any response has been converted into a marketing attribute, segment or score, because a person answering a research question has not agreed to have the answer sold as a targeting characteristic -- and that conversion is the exact step at which research becomes brokerage. (4) Sources and recipient categories. Offered the nil-result close.
