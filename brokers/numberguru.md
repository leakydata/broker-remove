## Gotchas

**Ask for removal in both directions.** A reverse-lookup index stores an
association, and that association is reachable from either end: number-to-name
and name-to-number. Removing one direction leaves the same fact findable from the
other, and a confirmation will not mention which was done.

**Every old number matters more than the current one.** This category is built
from numbers that have been disconnected or reassigned. A search of the current
number alone will miss most of what is held.

**Suppression is the whole question here.** Lookup indexes are rebuilt from
upstream feeds on a schedule. A one-time deletion is undone by the next refresh,
and the confirmation for a deletion reads identically to the confirmation for a
suppression. Ask which one happened, in those words.

**Do not forget the enrichment.** Carrier, line type, location and address data
attached to a number, plus any appearance of the name as a related person on
someone else's number page, all survive a removal scoped to the profile.
