## Gotchas

**Deletion is the third-most-important ask here, not the first.** In a lead
resale business a record is not one thing in one place: it was acquired from
somewhere, held, and sold onward to buyers who each hold their own copy. Deleting
the seller's copy leaves every downstream copy live. So:

1. **Provenance** — which publisher, form or supplier, and on what date. If
   consent is claimed, ask for the date, the form, and the wording shown. A
   consent nobody can produce is not a consent.
2. **Recipients** — name them, or give categories and a count, and direct each to
   delete. The count alone tells the subject the scale of what is already out.
3. **Deletion.**
4. **Suppression** on a do-not-resell basis — without it, the same lead is
   re-acquired from the same upstream source next month and sold again as new.

**Ask about aged and recycled inventory by name.** Leads that have been sold once
and are being re-offered at a lower price are frequently held in a separate store
from the live database, and are routinely missed by a deletion scoped to the
latter.
