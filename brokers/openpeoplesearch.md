## Gotchas

The four things this category never volunteers, and all four decide whether a
removal is worth anything:

- **Suppression or one-time removal?** The single question that determines
  whether today's confirmation still holds in six months.
- **Relatives and associates.** A profile-scoped deletion can leave the name
  listed as a "related person" on other people's profiles — still indexed, still
  searchable, and frequently the route by which someone is actually found.
- **The FCRA position**, reconciled against their own not-a-CRA disclaimer.
  Sites in this category disclaim consumer-reporting-agency status in their terms
  because that disclaimer serves a commercial purpose, then reach for FCRA
  scoping when a deletion request arrives. Ask them to reconcile the two rather
  than pick whichever is convenient.
- **Sources and recipients.**

Also ask whether the site shares an index with any sibling property. An unnamed
sibling is an unremoved one, and from outside a sibling and a stranger look the
same.
