## Gotchas

**The consumer site and the data product may be two different stores, and this
is the important half.** An opt-out on a consumer people-search site removes a
*profile page*. It does not necessarily remove the underlying record from a bulk
data product licensed to business customers through an API or a file feed.

If they are separate, a confirmation from the consumer site is entirely truthful
and leaves the commercial record being queried and delivered to customers. From
outside there is no way to tell. **So the first ask is the question:** does a
consumer-site opt-out remove the record from the data-as-a-service product, or
are they separate stores?

Three questions specific to a licensed data product:

- **Which identifier types do you match on**, and which of mine did you find?
- **Is there a standing suppression applied at ingest**, so a matching record is
  never re-published? Without one, deletion is a pause.
- **Do customers who already hold the record have to delete it?** If contracts
  require downstream deletion, ask them to confirm they have instructed
  licensees. If not, invite them to say plainly that delivered copies are
  permanent — that is a more useful answer than an implication.

Ask for derived attributes too: scores, household composition, income and
property estimates, and relative/associate links. The links between people are
independently searchable and are frequently how someone is located.
