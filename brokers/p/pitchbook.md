## Gotchas

address.** PitchBook's first answer read like a real search but was scoped to
the sending mailbox alone, ignoring the name/DOB/other-emails/phone/address
given in the letter body. Don't accept a from-address-only search as a full
answer — restate the other identifiers explicitly and ask again.

**A named human replies here, not a mailbox.** Worth keeping the correspondent's
name and title for any follow-up; a personal reply is more likely to actually
read a second message than a ticketing queue would.
