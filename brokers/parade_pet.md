## Gotchas

**A privacy address on one company's domain serving another company's brand is
a family signal** — second in the ranked list in `_BROKER_FAMILIES.md`, behind
only a shared non-obvious URL path. It usually means shared ownership or a shared
desk. Ask which entity controls the data and which other brands the mailbox
covers, and ask them to name the siblings.

**Publishers hold more than a subscriber row**, and a deletion scoped to the
mailing list misses most of it:

- newsletter, subscription, account and comment records — **including from
  properties since acquired, renamed or retired**, which is where old records
  survive unnoticed;
- the **hashed email** (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256) supplied to identity and advertising
  partners, plus cookie and device identifiers and any audience segment built
  from reading behaviour — and the *links* between them, not only the row with
  the name;
- **inferred attributes**: pet ownership, household composition, presence of
  children, income band, interest categories derived from on-site behaviour.
  These are what is actually monetised and they survive a contact-record
  deletion.

Include the cookie-opt-out pre-empt: a browser preference is not a deletion.
