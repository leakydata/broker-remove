# PeopleConnect family — Intelius, TruthFinder, InstantCheckmate, USSearch

- **Opt-out:** https://suppression.peopleconnect.us/login
  (`intelius.com/opt-out/` and the others all redirect here)
- **Method:** account-ish email-verified portal. **Free.**
- **Priority: 5.** One request suppresses reports across all four sites.

## Steps
1. Enter email → tick the Terms of Use / Privacy Policy consent box → **Continue**.
2. Click the verification link emailed to that address.
3. Search for your record in the portal and suppress it.

## Gotchas
- **Requires accepting a Terms of Use.** An agent should not tick that box on
  someone's behalf — it's a legal agreement. Fill the email, hand off the consent.
- **Does NOT cover Classmates.com**, despite Classmates being PeopleConnect. That
  needs a separate request on that site.
- Suppression applies to **name searches only**. Your data can still surface via
  phone, address, or email lookups, and on sex-offender lists. Worth knowing before
  calling this one "done".
- The tool is discretionary — PeopleConnect reserves the right to withdraw it.
- Free. If any flow asks for payment, it's the wrong flow.
