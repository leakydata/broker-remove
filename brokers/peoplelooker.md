## Gotchas

**Ask which entity operates the site and which siblings share its index, before
anything else.** People-search brands cluster: several front-ends querying one
store, with one privacy function behind them.

The asymmetry is worth stating in the letter itself, because it is what makes the
question reasonable rather than rhetorical: where several brands share an index, a
removal applied to one brand and a confirmation scoped to that brand are
**indistinguishable from a complete removal** — until somebody finds the subject
on a sibling site. The operator can see the shared infrastructure. The consumer
cannot. So only they can answer it, and asking costs a sentence.

Then the usual four: suppression versus one-time, every matching record rather
than one, relatives-and-associates scope, and the FCRA position reconciled with
the not-a-CRA disclaimer.
