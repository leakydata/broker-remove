   reply should say so plainly.
2. **Name the specific brand (Realtyverify) in the letter**, not just a generic

   named to route it correctly, same lesson as the cross-brand suppression
   cases in `_FAMILIES.md`.

  Realtyverify — don't assume it from the discovery alone.
