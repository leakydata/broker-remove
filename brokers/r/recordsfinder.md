## Gotchas

**Cite a peer that did it the better way.** This letter asked, in the
every-matching-record ask:

> *"If you can action this from the identifiers below rather than asking me to
> submit a profile URL for each one, please do — another operator did exactly
> that this week and it removed several records in one step where their own form
> removes one page per submitted link."*

Naming a live precedent from a competitor makes the better route **easy to grant
and awkward to refuse**, and it costs one sentence. It also reframes the request
from a demand into a description of normal practice.

Otherwise the standard six: suppression versus one-time removal; every matching
record rather than one; relatives-and-associates scope; criminal and court entry
disclosure with sources; **de-indexing with an explicit `noindex` directive** as
a separate item from content removal; and the FCRA position reconciled against
their own not-a-CRA disclaimer.
