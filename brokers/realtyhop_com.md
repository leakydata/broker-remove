For a people-search site the two are the same thing. For a property marketplace
they are not: a listing, an agent record, or an address-linked record can exist
without the person ever having interacted with the site at all.

So the follow-up worth sending names the remaining addresses explicitly and asks
the narrower question — *did you search only customer interactions, or also any
listing, agent or address-keyed records?* — while accepting the negative for what
it does cover.
