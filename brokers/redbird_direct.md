## Gotchas

**For a list broker, the whole request turns on one question:**

> **Will you pass this suppression through to the list owners you broker for, or
> does it apply only to files you own?**

A broker who suppresses only its own data leaves every brokered file untouched —
and for a brokerage, the brokered files *are* the exposure. A confirmation that
does not distinguish the two is worthless, and the distinction is invisible from
outside.

Pair it with: which parts are held as controller in their own right, and which as
manager of a client's list, with the clients named.

Otherwise the standard compiled-list shape applies — suppression at ingest before
deletion, source and consent wording, recipients with a direction to each to
delete, the sensitive selects named individually with the delete-rather-than-flag
distinction, and archived/aged/recycled inventory held apart from the live
database.
