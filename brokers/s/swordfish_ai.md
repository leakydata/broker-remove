## Gotchas

- **Profile URL deliberately NOT supplied.** See _SILENT_FAILURES 450a. The
  letter says so in terms: if they already hold one they do not need it from the
  subject, and if they do not, supplying it creates a new key. Do not hand one
  over here without asking the subject first.
- The product's whole value is finding a PERSONAL email and mobile from a
  professional presence, so that category is expressly in scope -- this is not a
  business-card record.
- **Contributed data is where a deletion gets undone.** If any part of the record
  came from a customer uploading their own address book, the next upload restores
  it. Suppression matters more than deletion.
