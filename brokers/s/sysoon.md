Two questions instead. (1) Do they hold anything about me as a LIVING person -- genealogy and death-record sites routinely hold data on survivors, informants, next of kin and people named in obituaries as mourners rather than subjects; if none, saying so closes it. People-search firms scrape sites like this and merge on common names; being recorded as dead propagates, is hard to reverse once in commercial datasets, and the person it happens to is usually the last to know. Asked for the LINK BROKEN, not their record deleted. DOB supplied deliberately for the opposite of the usual reason -- as the field most likely to RULE ME OUT of a namesake's record -- and asked whether their matching is name-only.
