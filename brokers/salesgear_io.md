(Singapore); policy effective 12 April 2021, pre-CPRA. Product: 'AI Sales Research & Hyper-Personalized Outbound -- five minutes of real research per contact, done by a machine, at sequence volume', plus a B2B contact database, a Website Visitor Tracker, a Free Email Extractor, and 'Salesgear Copilot + MCP for Claude & ChatGPT'. THE VECTOR WORTH THE LETTER -- their policy discloses: 'When you use the Software provided by the company, you allow Salesgear to access information in your email client and address book. We collect and store the email date, subject, recipients of mails and content.' So A PERSON WHO HAS NEVER HEARD OF SALESGEAR CAN BE IN SALESGEAR BECAUSE SOMEONE THEY ONCE EMAILED CONNECTED THEIR MAILBOX. The customer consented; the third parties in the address book could not have, and have no way to learn which of their correspondents installed it. Asked for that store to be searched SEPARATELY from the prospect database -- different store, different route, a prospect-DB query would not touch it -- and flagged [EMAIL] as the likeliest hit there, since an address book keeps whatever address a contact had when it was created. ALSO ASKED: pre-research/do-not-contact block applied BEFORE generation (if research runs at send time from live sources, deleting stored rows accomplishes nothing), citing Datagma and FullEnrich. AND ONE CORRECTION TO THEIR POLICY: they write 'We cannot do anything if your data has already been disclosed to our customers.' Accepted the practical point but noted 1798.105(c) frames the duty as NOTIFY AND DIRECT third parties to delete, and Art 17(2) similarly -- the obligation is to send the instruction, not to guarantee compliance. Their sentence tells a consumer nothing can be done when the one thing that can be done is the thing the statute requires. Offered 'we cannot identify which customers received a given contact' as a meaningful and different answer. Noted the CCPA B2B exemption expired 1 Jan 2023 and GDPR never had one, so 'this is B2B data' is the category the request is inside.
