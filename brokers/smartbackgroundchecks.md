## Status

- Current: `manual_required` (updated 2026-09-06) — never attempted; browser-only opt-out, email confirmation required
- Note: A standard people-search/background-check site. No published privacy email; the dedicated `optoutcheck` web form is the only route on record, and it requires an email-verification click to complete (`needs_email_confirm: true` in the registry).

## Steps

1. Go to https://www.smartbackgroundchecks.com/optoutcheck and search by name/state to find the listing (or listings — common names may return several records; identify the right one before opting out).
2. Submit the opt-out for the matching record(s).
3. A confirmation email with a verification link will follow — click it to complete the request. **Use a live mailbox for this, not one of the closed/dead search-key addresses.**

## Gotchas

- **Record identification is a manual step.** With a common-ish name, more than one listing may need to be located and opted out individually — do not opt out of a listing without confirming it is the right person (age, city, relatives match).
- **Email confirmation is mandatory** — the opt-out is void until the link is clicked, so this cannot be treated as done just because the form was submitted.

## Verification

Re-search the subject's name and last known city on smartbackgroundchecks.com after their stated processing window (typically a few days to two weeks) to confirm the listing no longer appears.
