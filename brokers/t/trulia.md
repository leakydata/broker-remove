## Gotchas

- **Zillow Group.** customerservice@ is the published contact; the letter asks
  them to forward to the group privacy team rather than return it, and to say
  where it went.
- **The occupant problem is acute here.** In a property database, suppressing an
  ADDRESS rather than the association between the address and the subject would
  remove whoever lives there now -- sixteen prior addresses, sixteen households
  who have not asked for anything. The letter says explicitly: if the suppression
  can only hold a bare address, apply it to the current one only.
- **Lead data leaves the building.** Any enquiry made through Trulia was likely
  forwarded to an agent or partner, and a deletion at Trulia does not reach a
  copy in someone's CRM. 1798.105(c) categories were requested so it can be
  chased onward.
