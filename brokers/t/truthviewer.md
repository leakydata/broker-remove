## Gotchas

- **Do not ask them to confirm corporate structure.** A support agent cannot
  answer it, and asking escalates the ticket to someone who will say less. Cite
  the sibling confirmations as precedent and ask only about *this* site.
- **The confirmation template is scoped to one hostname** and silently ignores
  a request framed as covering the platform — it does not refuse it, it says
  nothing. Read what the reply names, not what it implies.
- **It pre-blames your browser cache** for any residual listing, so verify with
  a cold fetch before writing again.
- **The site's own opt-out form has failed on at least one sibling**
  (`privaterecords.net` accepted input and did nothing on submit), which is why
  email is the preferred route here rather than the published form.
