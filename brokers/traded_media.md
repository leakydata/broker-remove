Traded reports commercial real-estate transactions -- closings, parties to a deal, broker rankings -- so the letter opens by saying it is NOT a takedown request: reporting on property transactions is journalism and much of the underlying material is public record. Same discipline as the Georgia Gazette letter. TWO NARROW QUESTIONS INSTEAD. (1) Is there a DATABASE or data product separate from the published stories? Their site offers search, rankings and market data, and a structured dataset of named individuals that is sold, licensed, exported or subscribed to is a different object from an article EVEN WHERE EVERY FIELD IN IT CAME FROM AN ARTICLE. That is the thing a broker listing could sensibly be about. (2) If a published item names me, IS IT ME -- a factual question, not a request. The subject is not a CRE broker, investor or principal and has never been party to a commercial property transaction, so a deal report carrying this name is somebody else's and I said I am not asking them to change a word. The narrow ask is whether any STRUCTURED field ties a namesake's item to my identifiers -- tag, profile, index, rankings row, syndication feed -- since transaction coverage is heavily scraped and a machine name-match does not know two people share a name. Discriminator: no real-estate or brokerage licence in any state, no connection to any commercial property entity. DOB offered to rule me out.
