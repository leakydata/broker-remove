## Gotchas

Four properties, one queue, one ticket — the consolidation works here, and a
named human replies rather than a template.

Arrest and warrant data is the category, which changes what a good outcome looks
like. **Deletion is not automatically the best result.** If a record exists and
is wrong, deleting it from this aggregator leaves the erroneous source record in
place to be re-ingested, and removes the evidence that would let it be
challenged. Ask what they hold and where it came from before asking them to
destroy it.

Where the record is accurate, deletion is straightforwardly the goal — but you
cannot tell which case you are in until they disclose. That is why the letter
asks for disclosure first.

Expect the FCRA-exemption deflection on any product they characterise as a
consumer report; `_DEFLECTIONS.md` has the reply.

date the removal took effect**. "Your request has been completed" across a family
of dozens of sites, with no list, is not a verifiable answer.
