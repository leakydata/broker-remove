## Gotchas

- **The third ask is the substantive one.** WHOIS history cannot be searched by
  person without a paid account, so the subject has no way to learn what exists
  except by asking. Do not treat "tell me what you hold" as boilerplate here.
- **Search old addresses, not current ones.** A registration made fifteen years
  ago carries whatever details were current then. A search of present details
  finds nothing and proves nothing.
- **Expect "it was lawfully public".** Answer twice: publication was a condition
  of registration rather than a choice about publicity, and the industry itself
  reversed the practice; then move to the stronger ground — republication and
  commercial supply *today* is a separate act.
- **Removing it downstream does not fix it.** The people-search page that
  surfaced this data licensed it from an archive. Discovery and fix are at
  different companies.
