## Gotchas

**Concede the legitimate retention up front.** A hygiene or suppression product
may genuinely need to keep a hashed form of an address so it is not re-added
later. Saying so first — and saying it will not be treated as a refusal — costs
nothing and makes the real ask sharper:

> What I am asking you not to do is keep the address in a product that is sold,
> while calling it suppression.

That distinction is the whole request for this category. Without it, "we retain
your address in our suppression file" is an answer that sounds like compliance
and may not be.

**Ask about the older addresses specifically.** Append and hygiene files are
built largely from addresses a person has stopped using, so a search of the
current address finds the least interesting record.

**Scope list used:** plaintext addresses; hashed forms as match or hygiene keys;
demographic, household and interest attributes appended to them; the edges
linking any address to name, postal address or phone; and any record of the
addresses in a validation, deliverability, hygiene or suppression product.

## Verification

Watch for a reply distinguishing suppression-file retention from saleable
inventory. If it does not draw that line, ask once, plainly.
