West Publishing Corp is the entity that holds the Thomson Reuters products that compile information from public and other lawful sources and make that information available to authorized customers for permitted purposes, including THE CLEAR AND PUBLIC RECORDS ON WESTLAW family of products.' Entity, register position and product family in three lines. That converts a third-party directory listing -- somebody else's judgement I could not check -- into a fact about their own corporate structure. THE FORK IT CLOSES: my original letter said I was not asking them to breach the FCRA, that a consumer report is not deletable on request, and that if they relied on 1798.145(d) I would ask which category it covered, since the exemption is ACTIVITY-scoped not entity-scoped. Thomson Reuters' own published positioning is that CLEAR is expressly NOT a consumer reporting product and not for FCRA-permissible purposes -- their limitation, not my inference -- so on that basis the FCRA exemption does not reach CLEAR data and the state-law rights apply undiminished. Put that as a proposition rather than a conclusion and invited them to name any part of the family that IS FCRA-regulated, which I would treat as out of scope and pursue under 609 separately. ASKED ONE PRACTICAL THING BEFORE USING THE PORTAL: does the OneTrust DSR portal route to West Publishing Corp and reach CLEAR and Public Records, or is there a separate intake? The failure being pre-empted is the group-level portal that accepts a request, returns a truthful completion, and never touches the subsidiary holding the file. Repeated the three confirmation asks a form cannot carry, with the third flagged as mattering more here than anywhere: public-records data is re-ingested continuously, so a deletion with nothing retained is rebuilt at the next refresh -- the courthouse record does not change, only their copy does.
