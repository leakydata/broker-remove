# Whitepages

- **Opt-out:** https://www.whitepages.com/suppression-requests
- **Support ticket:** https://support.whitepages.com/hc/en-us/requests/new
- **Verified email:** privacyrequest@whitepages.com  (`privacy@whitepages.com` also
  delivers but is not the published address)
- **Also operates:** 411.com
- **Priority: 5.**

## The key lever

Whitepages will tell you that only residents of states with consumer privacy laws
can make an "official" Consumer Privacy request, and list ~18 states. **That list
is not the whole picture**, and two things are worth knowing:

1. The same reply states: *"It currently is company policy to remove and not sell
   all information from our site, including public information, upon request."*
   That policy is **not limited by state of residence.** If you are outside their
   list, invoke the company policy rather than a statute — it is the basis that
   actually applies to you.
2. Their published list **omits California**, whose CCPA has been in force since
   2020. Treat the list as incomplete rather than authoritative.

They also confirm in writing that **an account is not required** to fulfil a
request, despite the standard flow implying otherwise.

## Gotchas
- Standard suppression flow ends in an automated **telephone verification** call.
  Ask whether it can be waived; they have an email path.
- Requests route into Zendesk (`support@whitepagesprivacy.zendesk.com`) and get a
  ticket number — record it, it's your reference.
- Ask explicitly for 411.com to be covered; it is a separate property.

Re-search whitepages.com and 411.com for the name + city after ~7 days.
